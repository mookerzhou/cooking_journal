We've been really into the Weilong Moishine Hot and Spicy Konjac Strips and I
thought to make these at home after reading [this article][konjac] about their
processing. It's not quite the same, but is still pretty good, has a nice meaty
tripe texture, and produces a lot less packaging waste than buying it at 99
Ranch.

Some things.
- Freezing/thawing the konjac makes it porous and crunchy.
- The rest of this is basically a Sichuan stir fry base. Did I ask Claude for
  advice about this based on the package ingredients? Definitely.

Steps:
- Freeze 16oz Konjac Tofu overnight, and then thaw the next day. Cut/shred into
  strips and then blanch in salted water (or steam). Drain, cool, and then
  squeeze out as much water as you can.
  * Can be purchased at Berkeley Bowl next to regular soy tofu
- In a wok, fry with some grapeseed oil, one at a time:
  * Sliced ginger
  * 1 tbsp whole Sichuan peppercorns
  * 1 tsp doubanjiang
  * 4 chopped garlic cloves
- Then add:
  * 2 tbsp chili oil
  * 1 tsp sugar
  * 1 tbsp Chinkiang black vinegar
- Toss in the konjac strips and let simmer for 10 minutes.
- Refrigerate!

You can definitely vary the amount of peppercorn, chili oil, for the amount of
Mala you're going for. I haven't tried steaming the konjac, which might be
better than blanching.
