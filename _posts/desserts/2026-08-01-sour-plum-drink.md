This is our home version of mixer for [Suan Mei Tang](original-recipe), using
ingredients that are easily accessible in Berkeley / 99 Ranch in Albany. It
probably should be diluted with sparkling water.

* 200g sour plums
* 50g dried apples
* 50g dried apricots
* 50g gogi berries
* 2.5L water
